## Everywhere

| Button | Does |
|---|---|
| **SELECT** tapped | next page: Play → Chords → Sound → Rhythm → MIDI |
| **SELECT** held | sound list + quick functions (below) |
| **START** | transport: drums, synced arpeggios, loop |
| **L3** | all notes off |
| **MENU** | quit (A yes, B no) |
| left stick | ↔ pitch bend · ↑ vibrato |
| right stick | ↕ brightness |

## MPK mini IV

**Pads, bank A** (hold a pad + play a key)

| Dim | Min | Maj | Sus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **6** | **m7** | **M7** | **9** |

Dim+Maj = augmented · Min+Sus = sus2 · 6+seventh = 13th · no pad: melody.

**Pads, bank B**

| 48 Key mode | 49 Next play mode | 50 Previous sound | 51 Next sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| **44 Drums** | **45 Loop: record** | **46 Loop: play/stop** | **47 Loop: undo** |

**Knobs**

| K1 | K2 | K3 | K4 | K5 | K6 | K7 | K8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Voicing | Bass | Amount | Tone | Chorus | Delay | Reverb | Volume |

Wheels: pitch ±2 · modulation = vibrato · pedal = sustain · program change = sound number.

## Looper

**Record** (at once, or on the bar if the tempo runs) → **record** again: the loop closes and plays
→ **record**: overdub a layer → **undo**: the last layer goes, then everything. Saved on exit.
